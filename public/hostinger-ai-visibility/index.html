Hostinger

AI visibility in web hosting

Research by Joe Robinson

Test date:  ·  questions  ·  AI platforms  ·  answers

I tested how often ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews included Hostinger in answers to web hosting questions. The questions were based on themes from 290 G2 reviews of Hostinger and 28 competitors, plus observed search questions. None named a brand.

The analysis records which hosts appeared, whether they were recommended, and which websites each answer cited.

Key findings

    What I would investigate next

    Check whether the gaps persist. Repeat the relevant questions to see whether Hostinger’s absence is consistent or varies between answers.

    Examine the answers and sources. Review how competitors are described on those questions and which pages are cited. This would give us specific product pages, comparisons and third-party coverage to examine.

    Review Hostinger’s relevant pages. Check whether they clearly explain the capabilities buyers asked about. Any proposed changes should follow that review; the results below do not establish why a host appeared or was absent.

    What the figures mean

    Included as an option:
    the answer presents Hostinger as a possible choice, whether as a top pick, another recommendation or a list entry.
    Rank in this test:
    Hostinger’s position among hosts included as options at least once.
    Recommended:
    the answer actively recommends Hostinger. This includes answers that make it the top pick.
    Top pick:
    the answer presents Hostinger as its single first choice.
    Hostinger website cited:
    the answer includes a link to a hostinger.com page as a source.

    Top picks are included in the recommendation figure, and recommendations are included in the overall inclusion figure. These percentages should not be added together. An answer can include more than one host.

    About this research

    • One answer per question on each platform. Results may change on a repeat run. Small differences between hosts should be treated cautiously.
    • Relevance assessed from providers’ websites. The websites were reviewed on 5 October 2026. Where a website did not confirm a capability, the host was excluded from that comparison. SiteGround’s EU website was used.
    • English-language questions. ChatGPT and Google were queried with US location settings. Google used desktop settings. Perplexity had no location setting. Claude was not included.
    • Questions based on reviews and search. The set contained questions, based on 290 G2 reviews and observed search questions. It did not include Reddit or forum research, or branded questions such as “Hostinger vs SiteGround”.
    • answers excluded from the assessment of hosts’ appearances and recommendations. Of collected answers, could be assessed reliably for how they presented each host. Unassessed answers were not counted as absences. Citation figures use their own answer totals.
    • This test records appearances and citations. It does not establish why a host appeared, why it was absent, or what effect an appearance had on enquiries or sales.
    • hPanel references are included. References to hPanel, Hostinger’s control panel, were grouped under Hostinger. Some Hostinger appearances may therefore refer to its control panel rather than name the hosting provider directly.
