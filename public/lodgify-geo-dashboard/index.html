V1 Limitations — important context

This is an early proof of concept. The most significant constraint right now is the number of prompts — each crawl only fired 10 test questions (5 non-branded, 5 branded), each asked 5 times. That's 50 data points per run.

In a production version, you could run hundreds of prompts covering the full range of things potential customers actually ask — different use cases, geographies, competitor comparisons, feature-specific queries, etc. The more prompts, the more statistically reliable the numbers and the more useful the per-prompt breakdown becomes.

The two crawls shown here were both run on May 24, 2026, a few hours apart — so the "trend" is more of a consistency check than a real time-series. Meaningful trend data would come from weekly or monthly crawls over time.
